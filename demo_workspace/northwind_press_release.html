Northwind Robotics Announces $45 Million Series C Funding

Portland, OR — September 14, 2026

Northwind Robotics Inc., maker of the Atlas line of warehouse robots and the Falcon line of inspection drones, today announced it has raised $45 million in Series C funding. The round was led by Cascade Ventures, with participation from Harborline Capital and returning investor Granite Peak Partners.

The company plans to use the funding to triple production capacity at its Portland manufacturing facility and to expand its engineering team in Austin, Texas from 40 to roughly 90 engineers over the next 18 months.

"This funding lets us meet the demand we're already seeing from logistics customers who want to deploy Atlas-7 at scale," said Priya Raman, CEO of Northwind Robotics. "We're also accelerating the Falcon-3 roadmap for the utility inspection market."

Northwind Robotics has now raised $78 million in total funding since its founding in 2019.

About Northwind Robotics

Northwind Robotics designs autonomous robots and drones for warehouse logistics and infrastructure inspection. The company is privately held.
